Naming Manager

Sentinel ▸ Validate

Reviews family and type names against the office naming rules and renames what you tick.

How to use it

  1. Click Validate ▸ Naming Manager.
  2. Read each row: current name, proposed name and why.
  3. Tick the rows to rename.
  4. Apply; the status line shows the ledger row.

Good to know

  • Duplicates are blocked, never suffixed.
  • Proposals are recovered from the name and measured width, never guessed.
