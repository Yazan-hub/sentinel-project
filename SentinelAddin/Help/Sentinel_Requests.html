Change Requests

Sentinel ▸ Coordinate

Coordinator review of pending modeller changes and ⚡ Fix proposals.

How to use it

  1. Click Coordinate ▸ Change Requests.
  2. Select a request and press Show to isolate the element.
  3. Approve to keep a change or apply a proposal.
  4. Reject to revert a change or drop a proposal.

Good to know

  • Only users listed as coordinators in %AppData%\Sentinel\settings.json can decide.
  • The audit trail travels with the model in Extensible Storage.
