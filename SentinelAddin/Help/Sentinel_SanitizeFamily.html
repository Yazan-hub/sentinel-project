Sanitize .rfa

Sentinel ▸ Validate ▸ Family Health

Audits an .rfa before loading: geometry budget, nested CAD, shared parameters, unnamed types.

How to use it

  1. Click Validate ▸ Family Health ▸ Sanitize .rfa.
  2. Pick the .rfa file.
  3. Read the audit; the family loads only on a pass.

Good to know

  • The solid budget is 150 solids.
