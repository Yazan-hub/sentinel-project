IFC Delivery Gate

Sentinel ▸ Validate ▸ IFC Gate

Exports and certifies an IFC against the project's delivery contract: PASS, FAIL or NOT CHECKED.

How to use it

  1. Make sure the model is bound (Project Setup).
  2. Click Validate ▸ IFC Gate ▸ IFC Delivery Gate.
  3. Export the active 3D view, or pick an existing .ifc.
  4. Read the verdict and the ledger line; FAIL means do not upload.

Good to know

  • A signed .sentinel-cert.json is written beside the IFC.
  • No contract installed means NOT CHECKED, never a pass.
