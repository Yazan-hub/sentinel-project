Publish Sheets

Sentinel ▸ Publish ▸ Publish

Renders all sheets to PNG for the web app's Sheets tab.

How to use it

  1. Click Publish ▸ Publish ▸ Publish Sheets.
  2. Wait for the progress window to finish.
  3. Open the web app's Sheets tab.

Good to know

  • Outside the governed IFC path: no version and no verdict.
