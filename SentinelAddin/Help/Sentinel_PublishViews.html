Publish Views

Sentinel ▸ Publish ▸ Publish

Publishes the views you choose to the web app's Views tab.

How to use it

  1. Click Publish ▸ Publish ▸ Publish Views.
  2. Tick plans, sections, elevations, 3D or drafting views.
  3. Publish; only ticked views appear on the web.

Good to know

  • Outside the governed IFC path: no version and no verdict.
