Review AI Proposals

Sentinel ▸ Coordinate

Reviews staged AI element proposals with the referee's verdict per element.

How to use it

  1. Click Coordinate ▸ Review AI Proposals.
  2. Pick a changeset and read each element's verdict.
  3. Tick the elements you accept.
  4. Apply; only ticked elements enter the model.

Good to know

  • Every decision is audit-chained on the ledger.
  • Ghost Builder, Photo Massing, Shell a Mass and Promote file their changesets here.
