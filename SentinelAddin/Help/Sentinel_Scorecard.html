Health Scorecard

Sentinel ▸ Validate

A weighted 0–100 compliance score with an A–F grade and per-domain breakdown.

How to use it

  1. Click Validate ▸ Health Scorecard.
  2. Read the overall grade.
  3. Use the per-domain breakdown to find where points are lost.

Good to know

  • Weights: block 8, request 4, warn 2, monitor 0.5.
