ROI Dashboard

Sentinel ▸ Standards & Build

Counts gate runs, renames and family heals from the project ledger, priced by the roi standard.

How to use it

  1. Bind the model (Project Setup).
  2. Click Standards & Build ▸ ROI Dashboard.
  3. Read the counts; money shows only with roi@n installed.

Good to know

  • Tools that write no ledger row are listed as not counted.
