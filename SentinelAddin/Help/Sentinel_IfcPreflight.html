IFC Pre-Flight

Sentinel ▸ Validate ▸ IFC Gate

Audits IFC export mappings and the contract's required properties before you export.

How to use it

  1. Click Validate ▸ IFC Gate ▸ IFC Pre-Flight.
  2. Read the summary dialog.
  3. Fix the IFC-01 / IFC-02 rows listed in the panel.
  4. Re-run until clean, then export or run the Delivery Gate.

Good to know

  • Generic Models and Specialty Equipment are warned: they export as proxies.
  • With no contract installed, required properties are listed as not checked.
