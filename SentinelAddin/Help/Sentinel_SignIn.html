Sign in

Sentinel ▸ Standards & Build ▸ Standards

Signs in with your Sentinel account so governed calls carry your name on the ledger.

How to use it

  1. Click Standards ▸ Sign in.
  2. Enter the same account you use on the web.
  3. Use Sign out to forget it on this PC.

Good to know

  • Without sign-in, calls use this PC's shared token.
