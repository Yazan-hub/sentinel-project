Scan Now

Sentinel ▸ Validate

Runs a full compliance scan against the document's active ruleset.

How to use it

  1. Open a project document (not a family).
  2. Click Validate ▸ Scan Now.
  3. Review the results in the Sentinel panel.

Good to know

  • The ruleset is re-resolved first; an unchanged one is a cheap check.
