Sentinel ▸ Standards & Build ▸ Model from Drawings

Reads storey counts and proportions from admitted photos and files a mass for review.

How to use it

  1. Click Model from Drawings ▸ 2b · Photo Massing.
  2. Pick where the mass stands (its footprint).
  3. Type one known height or width and press Scale.
  4. Review and file the changeset; apply it in Review AI Proposals.

Good to know

  • Photos give counts and ratios, not lengths.
