Apply Standard

Sentinel ▸ Standards & Build ▸ Standards

Loads a saved standards pack and builds it into the active model.

How to use it

  1. Open the blank or target model.
  2. Click Standards ▸ Apply Standard.
  3. Pick the standards pack and confirm.
