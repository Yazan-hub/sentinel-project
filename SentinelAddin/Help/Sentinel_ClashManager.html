Clash Manager

Sentinel ▸ Coordinate ▸ Clash

Native clash detection of linked RVT and IFC MEP against structure, graded Hard, Medium or Soft.

How to use it

  1. Load the MEP links (RVT or IFC) into the model.
  2. Click Coordinate ▸ Clash ▸ Clash Manager.
  3. Review the severity-coloured list.
  4. Show an element, export BCF, or create a 3D clash view.

Good to know

  • Clash views are named CO_MEP-CLASH_* and section-boxed.
  • Slow on big models; there is no cancel yet.
