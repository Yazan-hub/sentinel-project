Sentinel ▸ Standards & Build ▸ Model from Drawings

Creates levels and grids measured off imported section and plan drawings.

How to use it

  1. Import a section with a levels layer and a plan with a grid layer.
  2. Click Model from Drawings ▸ 1 · Datum from Drawings.
  3. Pick the layers and review the levels and grids.
  4. Create them.

Good to know

  • Do this before modelling any element.
