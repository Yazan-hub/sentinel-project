Show Panel

Sentinel ▸ Coordinate

Opens the dockable Live Coordination panel: rule pass rate, violation rows, ⚡ Fix buttons and the Doctor log.

How to use it

  1. Click Sentinel ▸ Coordinate ▸ Show Panel.
  2. Read the pass rate and the violation rows; BLOCK rows are listed first.
  3. Double-click a row to zoom to the element.
  4. Press ⚡ Fix on a row to open the fix dialog.

Good to know

  • Live scanning runs in the background; the panel shows its results as you model.
  • The Doctor strip lists benign Revit warnings Sentinel resolved for you.
