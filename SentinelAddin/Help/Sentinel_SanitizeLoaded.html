Sentinel HelpValidate ▸ Family Health

Heal Loaded Families

Sentinel ▸ Validate ▸ Family Health

Scans loaded families and silently injects missing shared parameters.

How to use it

  1. Click Validate ▸ Family Health ▸ Heal Loaded Families.
  2. Wait for the scan; healable families reload automatically.
  3. Review the families flagged as requiring human interaction.

Good to know

  • Geometry and CAD problems are flagged, never touched.
  • Each run writes one family_heal ledger row.
