Changes

The single tools that Fix opens.

In the panel's slide-out: Change Requests, Review AI Proposals, Applied AI Changes and MEP Openings.

Tools in this group

Change RequestsLists the change requests waiting for a coordinator and lets a lead or owner approve or reject each one.Review AI ProposalsLets you review AI-proposed model changes: see the referee's verdict per element, tick what you accept, and apply exactly that to the model in one Undo step.Applied AI ChangesLists every AI changeset applied on the project, says whether the saved model kept it, and lets you revert one, days later, without Revit's Undo.MEP OpeningsFinds where linked MEP crosses native walls, floors and structural framing and offers to place tracked provision-for-void markers or export a BCF.

Before you start

  • A project model open and active.

How to use it

  1. Open the slide-out, click Changes and pick a tool.

What you get

  • The chosen window opens.

Related tools

  • Fix — Proposed changes waiting for approval, and Undo for applied ones.
