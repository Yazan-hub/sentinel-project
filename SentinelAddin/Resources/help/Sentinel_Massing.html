2b · Photo Massing

Estimates a building's storeys and proportions from the project's evidence-pack photos, lets you scale and correct the numbers, and files the missing levels and a mass for review.

Photo Massing is for projects where you only have photos of the building. A vision model that runs on your own PC reads the admitted, blurred photos from the project's evidence pack and estimates storey count, height-to-width ratio, storey height, footprint and any openings it can see. These are an estimate: photos give counts and ratios, not lengths.

You supply one known dimension (the building's height or a facade's width, in metres) and press "Scale ▶"; the storey height and width are then worked out from it, while the depth is only assumed. You review every number in a window (amber fields are low confidence) and press "File for review ▶". That files one changeset on the project's ledger containing the Building Story levels the mass needs that the model lacks and one untyped mass between them.

Nothing is placed in the model by this tool. Review AI Proposals opens on the changeset and a person applies it there. After that, 2c · Shell a Mass turns the mass into typed walls, floors and a roof.

Before you start

  • A Revit project open, bound to a web project (Project Setup), in a plan view. In any other view you are asked to open a plan view first.
  • Not in a design option.
  • Photos in the project's evidence pack that have been admitted and blurred (Files ▸ Evidence in the web app). With none, the tool says so and stops. Photos whose blurred copy cannot be fetched or does not match are skipped with a reason in the pane's log.
  • The Sentinel bridge running, so the evidence pack can be read and the changeset filed.
  • A vision model on this PC (Ollama). Photos are never sent to a model off this PC; the tool refuses before reading anything.
  • No review window open and no other filing in progress; otherwise the tool tells you to finish or wait.

What you provide

Where does the mass stand?
First choice. "Pick its footprint" uses a closed CAD polyline, a property line or model lines you selected first. "Width × depth from the photos" centres a box on a point you click.
Known height (m) / Known width (m)
Type exactly one known dimension. With a picked footprint only the height row is offered.
Scale ▶
Works out the storey height and width from the known dimension.
Storeys, Height : width, Storey height (mm), Footprint width/depth (mm)
The estimate. Correct any value that is wrong; amber fields need your attention.
Rotation (°)
Turns the footprint counter-clockwise about its centre; 0 leaves it.
Opening rows
Windows and doors the model saw, with width and height. They are counted but not placed.

How to use it

  1. Open a plan view of the model and click Model from Drawings, then "2b · Photo Massing".
  2. In "Where does the mass stand?" choose the footprint option or the width-and-depth option. For the footprint option pick the outline; then pick a point for the centre if asked.
  3. Wait while the photos are read ("Reading photo i of n" in the progress window).
  4. In "Sentinel — Massing: review the estimate before filing" type one known dimension and press "Scale ▶".
  5. Check and correct the numbers and compare the storey count with the photos.
  6. Press "File for review ▶".

What you get

  • One changeset with the missing Building Story levels and a mass is filed on the project's ledger. Review AI Proposals opens on it by itself; a person ticks and applies it.
  • A receipt for the vision run is sent to the ledger. If a Photo Massing changeset is already waiting, that one opens instead of a second being filed.
  • If filing fails, a dialog says nothing was placed and nothing in the model changed.

Tips and limits

If no photo gives a reading, you get "Nothing was read" with each photo's reason.
A picked footprint must be one closed outline with at least three corners; otherwise the tool says why it cannot be used.
An older Photo Massing changeset that was read outside the evidence pack must be declined in Review AI Proposals before you run it again.
A model's storey count can be wrong in the same way on every photo; check it against the photos.

Related tools

  • 2c · Shell a Mass — Turns a selected mass into typed walls, a floor per level and a flat roof, one storey at a time, filed for review as changesets.
  • Review AI Proposals — Lets you review AI-proposed model changes: see the referee's verdict per element, tick what you accept, and apply exactly that to the model in one Undo step.
  • 1 · Datum from Drawings — Reads the levels from a section drawing and the grids from a plan drawing and creates them in the model, so that everything modelled afterwards sits on real heights and a real column grid.
  • Project Setup — Binds the open model to its web project and sets the project code, ghost source folder and Revit Doctor option.
  • 3 · Annotate Views — Creates the work-in-progress plan views the guideline prescribes for each level, pins levels and grids, and optionally copies view templates and makes sheets with tags.

Ribbon tooltip

Read a building's storey counts and proportions from the evidence pack's admitted, blurred photos (Files ▸ Evidence) - they give counts and ratios, not lengths - type one known height or width, press Scale, review the numbers, then file one changeset — the missing levels and a mass — for review; nothing is placed until a person applies it in Review AI Proposals; then Shell it (2c · Shell a Mass), one storey per level. It first asks where the mass stands - it picks its footprint (a closed CAD polyline, a property line or model lines) and where it stands.
