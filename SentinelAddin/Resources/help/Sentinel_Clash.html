Clash

A drop-down group holding the two clash tools: Clash Manager (run clash detection in this model) and Clash Register (read the team-wide clash register from the web).

The Clash button on the Coordinate panel is a pull-down with two commands that cover the two halves of clash work. "Clash Manager" is the authoring tool: it runs native clash detection inside the open model, grades each clash by severity, and lets you create a 3D clash view or export the clashes as BCF. "Clash Register" is the review tool: it shows the team-wide clash register recorded on the web, read-only.

Use Clash Manager when you are the modeller who wants to find and fix interferences between your structure and linked MEP or IFC models. Use Clash Register when you want to see the coordination picture agreed on the web (how many clashes are open and in which status) without leaving Revit. The register is filled by running Coordination ▸ Clash ▸ Run on the web with two or more models loaded.

Tools in this group

Clash ManagerDetects clashes between linked MEP/IFC models and your native structure, grades them by severity, and lets you open a coloured 3D clash view or export them to BCF.Clash RegisterShows, read-only, the project's team-wide clash register recorded on the web: how many clashes there are, their status counts, and the biggest by volume.

Before you start

  • Clash Manager needs loaded links in the open model; Clash Register needs the model bound to a web project and the bridge reachable. See each command.

How to use it

  1. On the Sentinel tab, in the Coordinate panel, open the "Clash" drop-down.
  2. Choose "Clash Manager" to detect clashes in this model, or "Clash Register" to read the web register.

What you get

  • Each choice opens its own dialog; the group itself does nothing.

Tips and limits

Clash Manager shows a Federation Gate line so you know whether the web's data gate has passed before you rely on the result.

Related tools

  • Clash Manager — Detects clashes between linked MEP/IFC models and your native structure, grades them by severity, and lets you open a coloured 3D clash view or export them to BCF.
  • Clash Register — Shows, read-only, the project's team-wide clash register recorded on the web: how many clashes there are, their status counts, and the biggest by volume.
  • BCF Issues — Sentinel's own issue tracker in Revit (no BIMcollab needed): a filterable, sortable list of the project's BCF issues with snapshots, quick edit and quick comment; zoom, section box and isolate; new issues from your selection; Fix in Revit; BCF files for other firms.
