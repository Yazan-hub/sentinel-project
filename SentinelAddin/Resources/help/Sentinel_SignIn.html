Sign in

Signs you in with your Sentinel account so everything Revit sends to the project carries your name.

Sign in uses the same e-mail and password as the Sentinel web app. Once signed in, every governed call from Revit (publishing, review, fixes, snapshots) is sent under your own session and the ledger records your e-mail. Signed out, Revit either uses this PC's shared machine token, in which case the ledger records the Windows user name rather than you, or, if no token is set, governed calls are refused.

The session is kept on this PC (encrypted for your Windows account) and survives restarting Revit. Sign out forgets it on this PC only; it does not sign you out of the web app. The password is used once to sign in and is not kept.

Some actions, such as installing a catalogue on the office, require you to be signed in as yourself because the bridge checks your role.

Before you start

  • The PC's Sentinel configuration (%AppData%\Sentinel\bcf-config.json) must contain the Supabase address and key. Without them the dialog says sign-in is not possible on this PC and the Sign in button is disabled.
  • You need an existing Sentinel account; there is no sign-up here.
  • The bridge address in the configuration must be one Sentinel accepts; if it is refused the dialog shows the reason.

What you provide

E-mail
Your Sentinel account e-mail.
Password
Your Sentinel account password. Press Enter or click Sign in.
Sign in / Sign out / Close
Sign in is shown when signed out; Sign out replaces it when signed in. Close just closes the dialog.

How to use it

  1. Click Sentinel > Standards > Sign in.
  2. Type your E-mail and Password.
  3. Click Sign in (or press Enter). The dialog shows "Signing in..." for up to about 8 seconds.
  4. On success the top line reads "Signed in as <your e-mail> - the ledger records your e-mail." Click Close.
  5. To forget the session on this PC, open the dialog again and click Sign out.

What you get

  • Your session is stored on this PC and used for every governed call from Revit; ledger rows show your e-mail.
  • Sign out removes the stored session on this PC only.

Tips and limits

Before signing in the top line reads either "Signed out - governed calls are refused until you sign in" or "Signed out - this PC's shared machine token is used, and the ledger records ... not you".
Failed sign-ins show Supabase's message (or "Supabase not reached") under the form.
If a stored session can no longer be refreshed, Sentinel signs you out and asks you to use Standards > Sign in again.
The dialog's footer shows which address it signs in at and which bridge it uses.

Related tools

  • Project Setup — Binds the open model to its web project and sets the project code, ghost source folder and Revit Doctor option.
  • Governed Publish — Publishes the whole model to the web project through the one governed path: IFC export, delivery gate, IDS and naming check, then a registered version on the ledger.
  • Build Office System — Reads the office standard out of the open "golden" model, lets you review and tick it, then builds it into this model and into the project's ruleset.
  • Review AI Proposals — Lets you review AI-proposed model changes: see the referee's verdict per element, tick what you accept, and apply exactly that to the model in one Undo step.

Ribbon tooltip

Sign in with your Sentinel account (the same as on the web): every governed call from Revit then carries your name on the ledger instead of this PC's shared token. Sign out forgets it on this PC only.
