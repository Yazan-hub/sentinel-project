2c · Shell a Mass

Turns a selected mass into typed walls, a floor per level and a flat roof, one storey at a time, filed for review as changesets.

Shell a Mass takes a mass in the model and cuts it at each Building Story level. For every storey it plans walls from that level to the next, with the exterior face on the mass face, a floor for the level and a flat roof on top. The types come from the office modelling guideline and the type catalogue installed on the project or its office. A curtain wall is used where a facade rule in the guideline says so, with the facade judged against True North.

It first shows you the plan in a dialog. Yes files it; No is a read-only run that changes nothing. Nothing is pre-ticked in the review that follows, and nothing is placed until a person applies it in Review AI Proposals.

A storey that leans or is not a single loop with straight edges is not planned; it is sent to a person with the reason shown in the dialog.

Before you start

  • A Revit project open (not a family), bound to a web project through Project Setup.
  • A mass in the model: an in-place mass, a mass family, a mass made as a direct shape, or the mass from Photo Massing once its changeset has been applied. Select it first or pick it when asked. With no mass at all you are told to model one, place a mass family or run Photo Massing. If no mass shows in the current view you are told to turn on Show Mass or select the mass first.
  • Building Story levels in the model that the mass crosses (Datum from Drawings can create them).
  • A guideline and a type catalogue installed on the project or its office. With no guideline, or no catalogue, the tool says so and plans nothing.
  • The Sentinel bridge running.
  • No other review window or filing in progress.

What you provide

The mass
Select one mass before clicking, or pick it when prompted ("Pick the mass to shell").
Yes / No in the plan dialog
Yes files the shell for review. No is a read-only run.

How to use it

  1. Select the mass (or leave nothing selected and pick it when asked).
  2. Click Model from Drawings, then "2c · Shell a Mass".
  3. Wait while the bridge is read in the background. Revit stays usable and the dialog opens by itself.
  4. Read the dialog: it names the standards used, lists the plan, says how many elements go into how many changesets, and lists the rows sent to a person (expand for details).
  5. Press Yes to file, or No to stop with nothing filed.
  6. Review AI Proposals opens on the first changeset. Tick the elements you want and apply.

What you get

  • One changeset up to 200 elements (one review, one Undo), or one changeset per storey above that. The first opens for review; the rest are listed in Review AI Proposals.
  • A receipt for the planning is sent to the ledger.
  • Rows sent to a person ride on the first changeset filed.

Tips and limits

If a Promote or Shell changeset is still waiting for review, it opens instead; apply or decline it, then run Shell a Mass again.
Once one storey is applied, walls stand inside the mass and Shell a Mass will not plan the rest of it. If only some storey changesets were filed, decline them and run it again.
If the mass cannot be shelled, the dialog says why and nothing is planned or filed.
Show Mass in the Massing & Site tab makes in-place masses visible so you can pick them.

Related tools

  • 2b · Photo Massing — Estimates a building's storeys and proportions from the project's evidence-pack photos, lets you scale and correct the numbers, and files the missing levels and a mass for review.
  • Review AI Proposals — Lets you review AI-proposed model changes: see the referee's verdict per element, tick what you accept, and apply exactly that to the model in one Undo step.
  • 3 · Annotate Views — Creates the work-in-progress plan views the guideline prescribes for each level, pins levels and grids, and optionally copies view templates and makes sheets with tags.
  • 4 · Promote (DD) — Promotes the model's walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, doors and windows to design-development types by the office guideline, type catalogue and LOD matrix, filing the changes for review.
  • Project Setup — Binds the open model to its web project and sets the project code, ghost source folder and Revit Doctor option.
  • Standards — A drop-down group for binding a model to its web project, signing in, and building, applying or ingesting office standards.

Ribbon tooltip

Shell the selected (or picked) mass — an in-place or loaded mass, or a DirectShape mass: it is cut at each Building Story level; typed walls (base level to the next, the exterior face on the mass face — a curtain wall a facade rule names, by Facade N/E/S/W against True North, is centred on it), a floor per level and a flat roof, typed by the exact rules of the guideline and type catalogue installed on this document's web project (or its office), filed for review as one changeset (one per storey above 200 elements). A storey that leans or is not one straight-edged loop goes to a person with why. Shows the plan first (No = read-only); nothing is pre-ticked, and nothing is placed until a person applies it in Review AI Proposals.
