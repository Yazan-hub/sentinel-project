Review AI Proposals

Lets you review AI-proposed model changes: see the referee's verdict per element, tick what you accept, and apply exactly that to the model in one Undo step.

AI agents, and Sentinel's own tools such as Promote, survey, drawing readers and Ghost Builder, do not edit your model directly. They file proposals (changesets) on the web project, where an automatic referee checks each proposed element against the project's requirements (IDS). Review AI Proposals is the Revit-side human gate: you decide what actually enters the model.

The command opens a picker titled "Sentinel — AI proposals waiting: <project>" that lists every proposal waiting for review, oldest first, each with its age, source, and its elements by referee verdict and web declines. A Promote storey is shown as one entry. Choose one and press "Review" (or double-click). A window "Sentinel — Review AI proposal: <name>" opens showing who proposed it, the referee's adjudication and the spec used, and one row per proposed element grouped by what it does (for example retype, create, reshape, rehost, type edit), each with a checkbox, a verdict badge and a "Show" button. Rows the referee accepted are pre-ticked; you can tick a rejected row to overrule it, with the failures on screen, and the result records that you did.

While the window is open, proposed new elements are drawn as a coloured ghost overlay in the model's 3D views; ticking and unticking recolours it, and it is removed on Apply or when you close the window. Survey proposals also offer "Show the scan" (the point cloud as crosses), and extra buttons to flag a stair or propose terrain from the scan.

The main button at the bottom reads "Apply N ticked in Revit". With nothing ticked it reads "Decline all (needs a reason)" and records a decline instead, which needs a reviewer note. Everything is reported back to the bridge and recorded on the audit ledger; what Revit applied is held on this PC until the bridge acknowledges it, so a result is never lost or applied twice.

Before you start

  • An open project document bound to a web project (Sentinel ▸ Project Setup); otherwise "This model is not bound to a web project — Sentinel ▸ Project Setup."
  • The Sentinel bridge must be reachable to list proposals; otherwise the picker says "Couldn't reach the bridge" with the reason.
  • To apply or decline you must hold contributor role or above on the project, signed in (Sentinel ▸ Sign in). A viewer, a non-member, or a signed-out user is refused before anything changes: "You are <role> on '<project>' — applying or declining needs contributor or above."
  • A rehost or a re-creation (replacing an element) needs a signed-in person, not the machine credential.
  • Only one review at a time: while a picker or review window is open, a result is still being reported, or Promote, Shell a Mass or Ghost Builder is working, the command shows a message that says to finish or close the window and run it again.
  • Proposals that create elements need the project's guideline (placement rules) to be readable; if it cannot be read, nothing runs and the proposals stay pending.
  • Each element's family and type must be loaded in the model; rows whose type is missing are marked, start unticked, and Apply refuses a ticked one.

What you provide

Review (picker)
Choose a proposal from the picker list and press "Review", or double-click it. Entries with an orange warning cannot be opened because a result for them waits on this PC.
Row checkbox
Tick to apply that element. Rows the web desk has declined are locked unticked and cannot be ticked here.
Show (per row)
Selects and zooms to the element, or zooms the active view to where a new element would be placed. Disabled for type edits, which have no place in the model.
Tick group / Untick group
Ticks or unticks every allowed row in that group.
Reason for the unticked here
Optional one line per group: why the unticked rows in that group are declined. Recorded for each of them with the result.
Reviewer note (recorded with the result; Decline all needs one)
Free text recorded with the result. Required when you decline everything.
Tick suggested
Restores the suggested ticks.
Untick all
Clears every tick.
Show the scan
Survey proposals only. Draws the survey's scan as crosses in 3D views, plans and sections; stays after Apply so you can compare the placed walls to the scan.
Flag a stair…
Pick the stair's two plan corners on the plan of a Building Story; the box is saved on the proposal as a flag for a person. Nothing is placed. Needs sign-in.
Propose terrain from the scan…
Survey proposals only. Reads the ground off the scan and files it as a separate proposal for one Toposolid of the office's type. Needs sign-in and Revit 2024 or later.
Retry report
Appears when a result could not be reported to the bridge; checks the model and sends it again.

How to use it

  1. Sign in (Sentinel ▸ Sign in) as a contributor or above, and open the bound model.
  2. On the Sentinel tab, in the Coordinate panel, click "Review AI Proposals".
  3. In the picker, read the list of waiting proposals and the line above it. Select one and press "Review".
  4. Read the header (who proposed it, adjudication verdict and spec) and any warnings above the rows.
  5. Go through the groups. Use "Show" on a row to look at it in the model, and the ghost overlay in a 3D view.
  6. Tick the rows you accept (or press "Tick suggested"). For rows you leave unticked, optionally type a reason for the group.
  7. Optionally write a reviewer note.
  8. Press "Apply N ticked in Revit". Or, to refuse everything, untick all, write the reason in the reviewer note and press "Decline all".
  9. Read the status and the dialog that follows: it states how many elements were applied, which were kept as they stand, any warnings, and what the bridge recorded (ledger row). If a report did not land, press "Retry report".

What you get

  • Apply creates, retypes, reshapes, rehosts or edits the ticked elements in the model inside one Undo step (a Promote storey is one Undo step for the whole storey). Elements Revit removed at commit, and ones it kept because the model had changed since they were read, are reported as rejected with the reason.
  • Unticked elements are reported to the bridge as rejected, with your per-group reasons; each changeset gets its own ledger row. A declined proposal changes nothing in the model.
  • After an apply that qualifies, Sentinel also sends a "LOD state after" row to the ledger. After applying a Photo Massing mass the dialog suggests the next step: select the mass and run Model from Drawings ▸ 2c · Shell a Mass.
  • Applied elements carry a provenance stamp so Sentinel can recognise them later. The result is saved on this PC until the bridge confirms it.
  • The pane's Doctor log records the outcome lines, especially if you closed the window before they arrived.

Tips and limits

If someone changes a proposal while your window is open, Apply re-reads it first. A proposal that is no longer "proposed" creates nothing; a ticked row that was declined on the web refuses the whole Apply and locks that row so you can press Apply again for the rest.
If Revit's transaction fails, the whole change set is rolled back and reported as declined with the reason; the model holds none of it.
If Revit does not finish the transaction in time, nothing is reported and the proposal stays proposed; check the model before reviewing again, because a second Apply could duplicate what Revit finishes.
A result that Revit applied but the bridge has not taken is kept on this PC and the proposal cannot be reopened until it is reported; the next Review AI Proposals checks the model and sends it. Results applied in another model wait for you to run the command in that model. Reports are also sent automatically when you open the model, provided you are signed in.
A Promote proposal may be offered alone with a note when another part of its storey is missing or waiting twice; applying it is then its own Undo entry. If a storey with type edits is declined, the dialog tells you to decline related storeys before running Promote again.
The ghost overlay is a display aid only; if it cannot be drawn the review still works and Show still zooms to a row.
Proposing a stair or terrain files a new proposal of its own for review; nothing is ticked or placed until you review it.
Closing the review window while a decline has not been reported loses that decline; the Doctor log says so.

Related tools

  • 4 · Promote (DD) — Promotes the model's walls, floors, roofs, ceilings, doors and windows to design-development types by the office guideline, type catalogue and LOD matrix, filing the changes for review.
  • 2 · Ghost Builder — Builds LOD 200 Revit geometry (walls, floors, doors, windows and more) from a 2D DWG plan, mapping CAD layers to Revit types by the project's standards, after you review and tick what to build.
  • 5 · Provenance — Shows where the selected element came from: Sentinel's provenance stamp in plain words.
  • 2c · Shell a Mass — Turns a selected mass into typed walls, a floor per level and a flat roof, one storey at a time, filed for review as changesets.
  • Sign in — Signs you in with your Sentinel account so everything Revit sends to the project carries your name.
  • Project Setup — Binds the open model to its web project and sets the project code, ghost source folder and Revit Doctor option.
  • Show Panel — Opens the dockable Sentinel pane, which shows the model's live rule results, the project's journey from the web, and the Doctor log.
