## 3. No EIR is issued

**We do not issue Exchange Information Requirements (EIR) with this tender.** An EIR is the client's statement of what information it needs, when, and how it will accept it. Without it the project is not following ISO 19650 at tender. Your pre-appointment BEP should anticipate our requirements and state plainly that the project is not following ISO 19650 until this is resolved with us (Part E p.24). After award we expect to write the EIR together with you, and to approve it before the BEP is confirmed.

## 4. What we offer as information standard, and what is missing

We offer one fixed value: **project code SNW**, to be used in every container name (a container is any named file, model or document).

Everything else is missing. We provide none of the following:
- an EIR (5.2.1);
- an information standard beyond the project code: no container name fields, status codes or classification (5.1.4);
- production methods and procedures (5.1.5);
- an information protocol, the contract terms for information (5.1.8; Part E p.10 says it belongs in the invitation);
- a BEP template or other shared resources (Part 2 p.42);
- a common data environment (CDE: the shared system where information is stored, reviewed and issued). The bidder proposes one.

Propose what you need under the BEP items E and F below. Any addition or amendment you propose is treated as a contract negotiation and made known to all bidders (Part E p.19).

## 5. Tender response requirements

Return one response with four parts. You may present them in one document or tool (Part E p.25).

1. **Pre-appointment BEP** (BIM Execution Plan: how your team will manage and deliver the information). Cover the seven items of 5.3.2 (Part E pp.25-26): A) named people for the information management function (the people who run information management for your team); B) information delivery strategy, including your task-team split; C) federation strategy (how the models are divided and combined); D) high-level responsibility matrix (who produces what); E) proposed additions to the production methods and procedures; F) proposed additions to the information standard; G) software, hardware and IT schedule. 2. **Capability and capacity**, per task team and as a team summary. For each team: ability and people to manage information, ability and people to produce it, and available IT (Part 2 p.43). Say whether the named people are available for the whole appointment (Part 2 p.39). Give evidence (Part 2 p.43). Third-party certification is one way (Part 2 p.43); past projects are another.
3. **Mobilisation plan**: each activity, who does it and by when, inside the mobilisation window. Include CDE access and workflow tests, export format tests, and team training (Part 2 p.45).
4. **Risk register**: one integrated register, not a separate one inside the BEP (Part 2 p.46). Cover assumptions (including our missing EIR), milestones, information protocol, delivery strategy, methods and standards, and mobilisation (Part 2 pp.46-47). Answer: how will you manage and reduce risk to information delivery? (Part 2 p.39)
