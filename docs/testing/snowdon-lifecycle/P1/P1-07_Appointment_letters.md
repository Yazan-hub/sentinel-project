SYNTHETIC, exercise only. Snowdon lifecycle test, not a real appointment.

**EXERCISE ONLY. These letters are not legal documents and create no rights or duties.** Save each as a .pdf outside Sentinel; Sentinel has no appointment record, so the line in BEP section 1 (P1-02 part (g)) plus these letters is the record. Legal validity is out of scope.

## What the reference file supports

- **5.4.6 (Part 2 p.54).** The appointing party completes the lead appointed party's appointment documents; the lead contributes. All five resources are included. Part 2 Table 3 (p.15) lists them for the lead's appointment: information standard, production methods and procedures, information protocol (the appointing party prepares); the EIR (appointing party); the BEP and MIDP (lead). Part E p.10: the protocol is included here.
- **5.4.7 (Part 2 p.55).** The lead completes the appointed party's appointment documents; the appointed party contributes. Same resources. Part 2 Table 6 (p.25): information standard, production methods and procedures, information protocol; the delivery team BEP; the lead's EIR; the TIDP (appointed party or task team prepares). The protocol is usually the lead's with relevant changes.
- **Incorporation clause and Information Particulars (Part E p.14).** The protocol takes effect only through an incorporation clause and completed Information Particulars, and it should take priority. Part E p.14 suggests clause text, which our reference file does not reproduce. So the exact wording of the clause, and the full list of what the Information Particulars hold, are **not verified**. The letters below name the clause with a placeholder, and complete the particulars only with facts from this exercise.
- **Executed appointments are the output of 5.4** (Part 2 p.95).
- Part E p.10 / the roadmap: no information protocol was issued in the tender (P0-01 section 4). In this exercise the lead drafts a short protocol summary of 6 bullets, outside Sentinel (roadmap P0 inputs), to be attached. Its contents are SYNTHETIC and not checked against any published protocol (not verified).
