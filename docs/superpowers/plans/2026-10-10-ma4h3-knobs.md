## Findings

1. **The held-out 1F F1 at 100 mm is 0.330, under the default bar of 0.6.** Every step raised it (0 → 0.330) and no d fell under the baseline. The bar is the founder's (open question 8). What remains is precision: faces that are not walls (furniture, fittings, a face with no partner kept as a wall).
2. **The attic's level error (+543.9 mm) is the reference's, not the survey's.** Under the attic are two large surfaces 496 mm apart: 418 181 mm (313 m², the 1F ceiling seen from below; 1F's walls under it) and 418 677 mm (382 m², the attic floor seen from above; the attic's walls over it), with few points between. The reference's attic floor samples looked within the band of the attic cut's guess and read the lower one; the survey's attic level is the floor. The attic has no traced walls, so no F1 changes. `reference.json` is kept as recorded (the protocol refuses any other); the next reference build takes the attic's floor from above.
3. **Deviation (MA-4e's verdict), measured with the reference walls** — 99 of 106 traced walls fitted from the cloud (each side's face its densest 10 mm bin within 400 mm of the centreline, 2.6 m tall from the floor; 7 had no face on one side), then placed as they are, shifted 20, 50 and 100 mm, and 50 mm too thick, through the bridge's `judge` as written (p95 ≤ 20 mm, coverage ≥ 0.25):

   | Case | Within | Out | Insufficient data |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | In place | 15 | 84 | 0 |
   | Shifted 20 / 50 / 100 mm | 0 | 99 | 0 |
   | 50 mm too thick | 0 | 99 | 0 |

   p95 refuses walls that are in place (median p95 182 mm: clutter and the scan's own surfaces within the 400 mm band). The other statistics on the same runs:
   - the signed mean does not separate anything (|mean| ≤ 20 mm: 59 % in place, 55 % shifted 50 mm);
   - **the share of points within 25 mm ≥ 0.4** reads 93 % of walls in place within, and 98 % of walls 50 mm off out — but 87 % of walls 20 mm off within, 81 % of walls 100 mm off out (a thin wall shifted by its thickness lands on its other face), and 52 % of walls 50 mm too thick out.

   **Recommendation (open question 8 is the founder's):** judge by the share within 25 mm at 0.4, and say in words that 20 mm cannot be told on this scan. Until the founder chooses, p95 and 20 mm stay.
